Questions clients ask first

Do you guarantee recovery?

No. Anyone who guarantees full recovery is not being truthful. We guarantee diligent analysis, clear reporting, and transparent communication.

Will you ask for my seed phrase?

Never. Legitimate recovery work does not require your seed phrase or private keys. If someone asks, stop and contact us for a second opinion.

What do Basic, Standard, and Premium include?

Basic ($320) covers a focused single-chain trace. Standard ($460) adds deeper hops, tagging, and a formal dossier. Premium ($1,380) is for multi-chain or ongoing monitoring — see the pricing section for full details.

How long does analysis take?

Initial triage is typically 2–5 business days. Complex multi-chain cases may take longer; you receive a written estimate before any paid phase.

Is this legal advice?

No. We provide investigative and technical services. Legal strategy should come from qualified counsel in your jurisdiction.
