How recovery works on the platform

Every case moves through the same recovery pipeline so you always know the stage of your file.

  1. 01

    Open your case

    Submit wallets, TXIDs, and what happened. We never ask for seed phrases or private keys.

  2. 02

    On-chain trace

    Recovery analysts map transfers, tag exchanges, and rebuild the timeline for your crypto assets.

  3. 03

    Recovery actions

    Exchange outreach, documented recovery reports, and next steps tied to your case file on the platform.

  4. 04

    Case updates

    Email and case status as new on-chain activity or partner responses come in.

Platform FAQ

What is VaultTrace?

A cryptocurrency recovery platform — you open a case, choose a plan when available, pay in crypto, and follow recovery work through our team and case updates.

Will you ask for my seed phrase?

Never. Crypto recovery on this platform does not require your seed phrase or private keys. Do not share them with anyone.

What do Standard and Premium include?

Standard ($460) covers a focused trace with exchange tagging and a case report when that scope is open. Premium ($1,380) adds multi-chain work, a dedicated analyst, and 30-day monitoring. If checkout says a plan is not available, choose another plan on the pricing page.

How long does a recovery case take?

Initial work usually starts within a few business days after payment is confirmed. Complex multi-chain cases can take longer; your case page will reflect status as work progresses.

How do I pay?

Select a plan, enter your name and email, then send the plan amount in ETH or USDT on Ethereum mainnet. Confirmation typically takes 10–20 minutes before your case is activated.
